## From the first idea to everyday use

JustLAB is an independent digital studio founded by **Justin Neubert**. We bring product design, software development, and platform engineering together to turn ideas into useful products—and keep them running after launch.

We build our own tools and work directly with clients on websites, custom applications, automation, and cloud infrastructure. What we learn from building and operating our products feeds into every project we take on.

## Built in the lab

### [JustCD](https://github.com/JustLABv1/justcd) · Kubernetes delivery from Git

A Git-driven delivery control plane for Kubernetes. Render YAML, Kustomize, and Helm, review drift plans, and apply approved changes with scoped credentials. JustCD runs outside your managed clusters, with no JustCD operator or CRDs required.

### [JustAI](https://justlab.app/en/products?product=justai) · AI with your context

An AI workspace that brings chat, knowledge search, transcription, and MCP tools together on your own infrastructure. Connect your documents and tools so AI can work with the knowledge your team already has.

### [JustScan](https://justlab.app/en/products?product=justscan) · Container security

Find vulnerabilities in container images and Helm charts before deployment. JustScan helps engineering teams understand risks and identify packages that need attention before the next release.

### [JustApps](https://justlab.app/en/products?product=justapps) · Your internal app catalogue

A self-hosted home for discovering, organizing, and sharing internal software. Bring application descriptions, ratings, and deployment guidance together so teammates can find the right tools and understand how to use them.
